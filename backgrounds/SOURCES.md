# Wallpaper sources

All six wallpapers are AI-generated with Codex's image generation in a flat gouache style,
seen from above, after the look of the cover of Robert Johnson's *King of the Delta Blues
Singers* (Columbia, 1961). The cover itself is not included. The room (wall 4) was the style
reference for the rest of the set. Each wall was matched to the same mean lightness, chroma and
hue in OKLab, then upscaled 4x with Real-ESRGAN (Upscayl's digital-art model) and fitted to
3840x2160. The crossing's shadow was rebuilt from a side silhouette of the same pose. Saved as
lossless PNG.
